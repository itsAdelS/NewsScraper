Link Collection
Collect hyperlinks from:
Anchor tags
Article cards

Bulletin listings

Resource lists
URL Handling
Convert relative URLs into absolute URLs.
Preserve discovered URLs exactly.
Remove duplicate URLs.
Ignore mailto links.
Ignore javascript links.
Ignore social media links.
Ignore site navigation links.
Filters
Exclude:
Archive pages
Category pages
Search pages
Contact pages
Login pages
Home pages
Pagination controls
Date Filtering Logic
Determine article dates from:
Visible date text
Nearby sibling elements
Article metadata
Listing containers
Include an article if:
Its date falls within the specified target month and year.

Playwright Requirements
The endpoint should:
Use Playwright.
Wait for page content to load.
Support JavaScript-rendered content.
Scroll the page if necessary.
Collect links after rendering is complete.
Timeout gracefully.

Special Requirement
Do NOT use AI or LLM reasoning for URL extraction.
Use deterministic HTML parsing wherever possible.
Playwright should gather the rendered DOM.
Extraction should be based on:
href values
DOM structure
date patterns
The endpoint should only use heuristics when necessary to associate dates with nearby links.
Goal: return reliable article URLs across payer sites such as Anthem, BCBS plans, CareSource, Molina, Aetna, UHC, and Humana without depending on generative AI.
